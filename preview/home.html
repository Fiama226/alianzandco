Who we are

20+ years of engineering Africa's most ambitious projects

Alliance & Co is a multi-disciplinary engineering and construction company headquartered in France, with operations across West, East and North Africa. We exist to deliver complex projects that drive the continent's development — safely, sustainably, and to the highest international standards.

  • ISO 9001 / 14001 / 45001 certified
  • Active in 18 countries across Africa
  • EPC, EPCM and O&M delivery models
  • Strong local content and partner network
