A professional, production-ready WordPress theme for Allianz & Co — an African
engineering & construction company. Inspired by the Avelon visual system: bold
typography, dark/orange accents, large hero sections, modular content blocks.

== Features ==

* Custom post types: Team, Services, Projects, FAQs
* Customizer-driven homepage (hero, stats, contact info, social links)
* Built-in contact form (no plugin required)
* Accordion FAQ, animated counters, scroll reveal
* Custom logos for light & dark headers
* One-click content seeding on theme activation
* Translation-ready
